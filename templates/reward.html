What is checked, and who pays

Checked: the basket is what you paid for through PayPal; steps come from your phone's health platform. Not checked: meals cooked are your word, so they weigh least. Whoever games them still only gets back a share of money really spent on groceries.

Who pays: in this demo, the platform. In the product, whoever gains when you do your part: your employer inside its welfare plan, your insurer, the retailer whose basket becomes weekly and loyal.
