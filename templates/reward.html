What is checked, and who pays

Checked: the basket is what you paid for through PayPal; steps come from the health app on your phone (sample data or typed by hand in this demo). Not checked: meals cooked and training sessions done are your word, so they weigh less. Whoever games them still only gets back a share of money really spent on groceries.

Who pays: in this demo, the platform. In the product, whoever gains when you do your part: your employer inside its wellness plan, your insurer or a food-is-medicine program, the grocer whose basket becomes weekly and loyal (merchant-funded cash back, as PayPal offers already work). PayPal moves the money; it does not fund the reward.
